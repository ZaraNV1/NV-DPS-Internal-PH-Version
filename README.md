# NV-DPS-Internal (prototype)

The Digital Product Studio's internal platform for reviews and financial visibility. A front-end prototype of the internal platform described in `Draft_PRD.docx` (v3.0) and the BA wireframes: role-based dashboards for Admin, Practice Head, PM, PO, Senior BA and BA .

## Run it

Needs Node.js (any recent version). No install step, no dependencies.

```bash
node serve.js
```

Open http://localhost:5173. You need an internet connection for the web fonts and the animation library (GSAP), which loads from a CDN.

## Signing in

There are no real passwords. On the login screen, pick a role under "Sign in as" (it fills the email and password) and press Log in, or type any colleague's work email (`firstname.lastname@newvision-software.com`) with any password. The app is light-only for now: dark mode is parked (set `DARK_MODE` to `true` in `js/ui.js` and remove `data-theme="light"` from `index.html` to bring back the sun/moon switch).

- **Real (from the practice's employee sheet and org chart):** names, employee IDs, designations, levels, primary skills, financial target flags, reporting managers, joining dates, locations, clients, projects, project codes and photos. Blanks in the sheet show as "Not set". Brian Okuno isn't on the sheet and keeps his org-chart details.

Rupees convert to US dollars at ₹96.48 to the dollar, the market rate on 5 Oct 2026 (`RM.FX_USD` in `js/data.js`); billing rates and the Practice Overview use it. These are labelled "Sample" in the UI.
- The prototype's clock is fixed at **1 Oct 2026**. Quarters follow each person's joining date, and a quarter's marks open on the 1st of its last month and are due on its last day, so the quarters open for marks are the Aug–Oct ones (everyone who joined in February, May, August or November), opened that morning and due 31 Oct; October's monthly review is open for everyone. Reviews start at go-live, **Apr 2024**.
- All changes you make are saved in your browser's localStorage only. Use "Reset demo data" in the account menu (your name, top right) to start over.

## Access rules

- **Organization chart and profiles:** every role can open the Organization map. Admin and the Practice Head can open anyone's profile; everyone else can open their own and their direct and indirect reportees' profiles (other names show as plain text). Review status and financial figures inside a profile stay limited to each role's own scope (below). A person's card on the map shows their **average rating** for the financial year, manager and self, each with an arrow against last year's (up, down or level), for anyone who can see their reviews; it opens their review History.
- **Editing people records:** Admin only. Edits are audited under the editor's name. The Practice Head can view every record but not edit it; the one change they can make is their own photo, from their profile (Upload a photo / Remove photo). It's kept in the browser with the rest of the demo data and shows wherever their avatar appears.
- **Resource Allocation:** Admin and Practice Head only.
- **Financials:** Admin, Practice Head and PM only. PMs see their own figures and their direct and indirect reportees'. POs, Senior BAs and BAs see no financial figures.
- **Reviews:** a quarter's manager marks are given by the person's manager when its marks opened, unless Admin moved them to someone else. There are two tiers. **Full** (monthly reviews, evidence, marks, comments and the appraisal summary): Admin, the Practice Head, and the person's manager and every manager above them. **None:** anyone else. Someone Admin moved a quarter's marks to sees just that quarter (its marks and its months' monthly reviews), not the rest of the person's review. **The employee:** their own in full, with the manager's feedback once it's done and the manager's marks once they're final; they don't see appraisal summaries. The rules live in `RV.access`, `RV.recAccess` and `RV.monthAccess`.

## The layout

The menu is a panel on the left with the pages for your role. It's closed by default, so the page takes the full width: the **menu button** at the top left opens it, and the same button, now at the top of the panel, closes it again. This browser remembers when you leave it open. On a narrow screen it slides in over the page instead. The top left holds the menu button (while the panel is closed), then the New Vision logo, drawn straight on the page, with the app's name beside it when there's room (it leads to your landing page), then a hairline and where you are. As the panel opens the button folds away with it, so the logo and the trail glide over; on a narrower screen the name folds away too. The top right holds search, notifications and your account (photo, name and role: My profile, Preview another role, Reset demo data, Sign out).

## Reviews

The review screens, in plain words. The rules live in `js/review.js` (`RM.RV`); the screens in `js/views-review.js` (Upcoming Appraisals in `js/views-me.js`); the templates in `js/data.js` (`RM.GOALSHEETS`).

- **Quarters follow the date of joining.** Each person's review year starts in the month they join and runs twelve months. Its quarters are that month and the next two, then every three months, so Q4 ends the month before the anniversary month and the anniversary month starts the next year. Someone who joined on 22 Sep 2025 has Q1 Sep–Nov, Q2 Dec–Feb, Q3 Mar–May and Q4 Jun–Aug, then their appraisal on 22 Sep 2026. The joining quarter always counts as a full quarter (no one joins mid-quarter). People who were already here at go-live keep the quarters of their own joining date; quarters that started before go-live have no marks. Quarters are written with their months (Q4 Jun–Aug), since everyone's are different. The financial year (April–March) is kept only for practice-wide figures, which count a quarter in the year it ends in (`RV.quarterFor`, `RV.year`).
- **Review** (`#/me/review` for your own, `#/review/<id>` for someone else's): one page per person, with three tabs.
  - **This month:** the month's **monthly review**, two parts side by side. The employee adds their work and progress, achievements, projects discussed, challenges and the review deck link; their manager adds feedback, follow-ups, the meeting date (in that month, and not after today once held) and whether it happened. Both are due by the end of the month; anything not done is marked **missed**. Below it, the **evidence**: a table under each goal, grouped by the template's five categories. Rows added this month can be changed until it ends; each table shows its three latest earlier rows (the rest a click away), so the record reads as one continuous list.
  - **Marks:** a quarter's marks. It opens on the quarter waiting on you, with a switch for the latest quarters. The employee marks each goal out of its maximum, adds comments and value adds, and sends the marks to their manager; the manager can ask for changes (the first version is kept) or add their marks, which makes them **final**. Statuses: Not started, Draft, Changes asked, **With manager**, Final. Marks open on **the 1st of the quarter's last month** and are due on **its last day** (a Jun–Aug quarter: open 1 Aug, due 31 Aug); not final by then, they're **missed**, and they can still be finished. The quarter's monthly reviews and evidence sit underneath, as one table and one record, for reference. Switching tabs keeps unsaved changes; leaving the page asks first.
  - **History:** each review year (Sep 2025 – Aug 2026, say) with its chart, its four quarters with self and manager marks, and (expanded) the year's monthly reviews and evidence. Managers, the Practice Head and Admin also see the year's appraisal summary, the next appraisal and every summary so far. Admin sees the person's **form changes** (template changes, fields added).
- **Team** (`#/me/team`, managers) and **Reviews** (`#/admin/reviews`, Admin): one row a person, with what each is **waiting on** (whose marks, and whether they're missed), this month's monthly review, the last quarter's marks and the next appraisal. Filters: Everyone, Needs you, Missed, Marks open, Appraisal in 90 days. A manager's list is everyone below them (direct and indirect). Admin's adds the on-time figures, appraisal summaries waiting on missed marks or not created, and on-time by due date and by manager, and Admin can move a quarter's marks to another manager from the row. Since quarters are each person's own, the practice's figures group marks by the month they're due: this month's (the quarters ending this month) and last month's, on time. Opening a row goes to that person's Review page, on the tab that needs attention.
- **Practice Reviews** (`#/ph/reviews`, the Practice Head): how the practice is rated this year, and what's still open this month. Nothing on it is the Practice Head's to do, so it has no "Needs you", no "You" and no menu count. Two cards: the **average rating** for the financial year, manager and self side by side (each final quarter's marks as a rating, averaged per person and then across the practice; a quarter counts in the financial year it ends in), each with a small arrow and the change against last year (green up, red down, a grey arrow alone when level; no chip), and last year's figures beneath; and this month's **monthly reviews pending**, out of everyone in the practice (26 / 32 in the sample), its line leading with how many aren't in reviews (6), then the due date at the month's end. Then everyone in the practice (32), A to Z: their manager, their **Last appraisal rating** (out of 5, from the salary history as on the profile; its note reads "Compared to the year before"), with the same small arrow and change against the appraisal before it (hover for both dates and ratings; no arrow with only one appraisal so far, a dash with none yet), and the next appraisal (the coming anniversary's date only, no badge). Above it, Resource Allocation's toolbar: a search that finds a person by name, ID or manager, Billable | Non-billable, role, client, location, project and how many people match; the filters stay set while you open a profile and come back. The six not in reviews (the two contractors, and four people whose designation waits for Admin to map it to a review role) show **Not in reviews** under Next appraisal, with the reason on hover; the cards still count only the people in reviews. A row, or the name in it, opens that person's employee profile on its **Reviews** tab (`#/people/<ID>?tab=reviews`), and Back returns here. For the Practice Head the separate Review page for someone else now goes to that tab too (its address, History and This month); it stays only for what's theirs to do there: a quarter's marks (each quarter's row on the Reviews tab opens them) and This month for a direct reportee whose monthly feedback they add. On such a reportee's Reviews tab a line says what's waiting on them, with **Give marks** and **Add feedback** links. The Practice Head isn't notified about marks waiting on them.
- **Marks to rating** (`RV.rating`): a quarter's marks, as a share of the quarter's total, on the 5-point scale. Within a band the rating rises evenly with the marks, to one decimal.

  | Marks | Rating | Band | Increment |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | 95+, with a financial target that's been met | 5 | Exceptional Performance | 14% or higher |
  | 95+, otherwise | 4.6 to 4.9 | Outstanding Performance | 12% to 14% |
  | 90–94 | 4.3 to 4.5 | Exceeds Expectations | 10% to 12% |
  | 80–89 | 4 to 4.2 | Strong Performance | 8% to 10% |
  | 70–79 | 3.5 to 3.9 | Meets Expectations | 8% and below |
  | Below 70 | 3.2 and below (1 at 0) | Performance Improvement Required | PIP |

  A financial target is met once the lead's cluster's uploaded actuals for the year reach it (Financial Performance's figures, this year only, so an earlier year's 95+ is at most 4.9). An average between two bands' ranges counts in the lower band. The appraisal summary still shows marks, not ratings.
- **Upcoming Appraisals** (`#/ph/appraisals`, the Practice Head): just the title, then a month calendar of joining anniversaries (each with its summary's state, or what the next one is expected to be), beside the list of appraisals in the next 30 days, 90 days or year.
- **Appraisal summary** (`#/appraisal/<id>`): on each joining anniversary, from **that review year's four quarters**. Q4 ends the month before the anniversary month and its marks are due on its last day, so the summary is created on the anniversary if all four are final by then, otherwise as soon as the last one is: until then it's **Waiting on marks**, as they were missed; a quarter is never skipped and never moves to another year. In the year reviews started here (go-live, Apr 2024), three quarters still make a summary (marked "3 quarters"); fewer don't. It shows the quarters' marks, the self and manager averages side by side (no combined score), both sides' comments, and the year's monthly reviews and evidence. A summary still waiting on marks already shows that year's monthly reviews and evidence. **Print or save as PDF** opens every collapsed part. Employees don't open their own.
- **Behind the screens:** each person has a running record of monthly reviews and evidence for each review year, from its first month (or go-live) to the month before the anniversary month, so it holds the year's four quarters. It closes with the year, whatever happens to the summary (made, waiting on a quarter's marks, or not made), and a fresh record starts on the 1st of the anniversary month. This month's evidence only shows rows since then; earlier ones are in History and on the summary. The screens never show these records' numbers. Fields added mid-way (a PO's first reportee adds the reportee fields) and template overrides apply from the start of the person's next quarter; earlier months stay as they were.
- **Templates (Review setup):** from the four Quarterly Goal Review decks: **BA & Sr BA** (18 goals), **PM** (23), **PO without direct reportees** (24) and **PO with direct reportees** (25). Every template is version 1 and read-only here. The role picks the template; an **override** gives one person another template from the next quarter, with a reason, and never changes their role. People whose designation isn't mapped to a role (the UX titles) aren't in reviews until Admin confirms the mapping (with an Undo until anyone adds a monthly review or evidence).
- **Corrections:** final marks change only through an Admin correction with a reason. It makes a new version of any appraisal summary that used them; earlier versions stay readable and every change is audited.
- **Notifications:** in-app, by event and audience (marks open, due, sent, changes asked for, final, missed, upcoming appraisal); Admin can mute each one in Settings. Due and missed notifications are reminders: once read, they come back after the reminder frequency in Settings, for as long as they're outstanding.
- **Words:** the screens say Draft / With manager / Final / Missed. In the data and code they're still `Draft`, `With Reviewer`, `Locked` and `RV.incomplete`; a quarter's marks are a "goal sheet" record (`db.quarters`), the running record is a "form" (`db.forms`) and the appraisal summary is an appraisal record (`db.appraisals`). Stored history and audit text written in the older words is shown in the new ones (`RM.plain`, `RM.plainAction`).
- In the sample data on 1 Oct 2026: the Aug–Oct quarters, whose marks opened that morning, are at every stage (Aaditya's and Yogiraj's not started, Zara's and Chitansee's in draft, Shweta Achchha's sent back with changes asked, Pooja's with Shine, Shruti's with Pooja, who has saved part of the marks, Borad's moved from Talha, on planned leave, to Kuldeep, Rehan's already final); everyone else's latest quarter ended on 31 Aug or 30 Sep and is final; four September monthly reviews were missed; Nikhil never sent their Q2 Jun–Aug marks, due 31 Aug, so they're missed; Yogiraj's Q4 May–Jul marks, due 31 Jul, were never made final, so they're missed and the summary for the 29 Aug appraisal waits until Prakhar finishes them (the Aug 2025 – Jul 2026 record still closed with its year, so a fresh one started on 1 Aug); Shruti's Q3 Feb–Apr was made final late (6 Jun, due 30 Apr); Tejas's first summary (22 Sep) was created on 25 Sep, when their Q4 Jun–Aug marks, due 31 Aug, were made final late; five people's first summaries here (Rehan's, Dhaval's, Archana's, Kuldeep's and Shweta Gupta's) used three quarters, as reviews started part-way through their year; Kopal gained the reportee fields from June and Manisha from July, the start of their next quarters.

## Revenue, cost and profit

Every financial view works out the year (April to March) the same way, for the whole practice or for one team:

- **Revenue** = each person's actual for a month once it's uploaded, plus their forecast for the months where it isn't. An actual is never more than its forecast: Financial Updates and Fill from CSV refuse one that is, and the sample data keeps to it. There are no locks: a figure counts as soon as Admin saves it. A month part-way through its uploads counts the uploaded actuals and the forecasts for everyone else. In months nobody has figures for yet, each person's latest forecast carries forward to March (run-rate).
- **Cost** = (CTC + ₹3 lakh) for each person, from the month they joined, added up: each person costs (CTC + ₹3 lakh) ÷ 12 for every month of the year they're in the practice (the joining month counts in full; `RM.fyShare`). CTC is the person's current annual CTC from their salary history. The ₹3 lakh is `RM.OVERHEAD` in `js/data.js`.
- **Profit** = revenue − cost. The margin shown with it is profit ÷ revenue. A month's profit is its revenue less a twelfth of the year's cost for everyone in the practice that month.

The sample year is a pessimistic one: actuals land under their forecast (85–100% of it) and the leads' targets are stretch targets, so the practice and most clusters finish short. Prakhar's cluster is the optimistic exception: its actuals come in at or near the forecast and the forecast clears its target. On 1 Oct 2026 the practice has achieved $580K of a $1.29M target, $712K remains, the forecast for Oct – Mar brings $661K, and the gap is $51.0K; Talha's cluster has a $34.1K gap ($168K remaining, $134K forecast); Prakhar's has none (a $20.7K surplus).

The **practice** is everyone active in it. It has no budget; its target is the sum of the leads' targets (below). A **cluster** is its lead plus all their direct and indirect reportees.

There are no per-person goals. Only the leads the employee sheet gives a financial target (Prakhar, Komal, Talha, Pooja, Kuldeep and Pratik) have a **Target**: one revenue figure for the year. Admin sets targets on Financial Updates, and changes are audited. A team whose lead has a target shows it as a fourth figure beside Cost, with revenue vs target and a marker on the year bar. Targets live in `db.targets`.

The **DPS Financials** card is the same on the Practice Overview, Financial Performance (the practice, or the cluster picked in the drill path) and Team Financials (a PM's cluster), always in US dollars (`RM.dpsCard`): Revenue, Cost (Practice cost or Cluster cost), Target and Profit for the year, then Target progress and the monthly target and profit (below). A cluster whose lead has no target shows revenue, cost and profit with revenue and profit by month instead. A PM's My Reporting Team page shows their team's revenue, cost and profit (and target) as tiles, in rupees.

My Financials lists every month that has figures, including one still being uploaded. Financial Performance and Team Financials have no month switch: their breakdown adds up every month with figures (the year so far), with billability from the latest month. A person with no actual yet shows "Not uploaded". Actual − forecast covers the uploaded actuals only and is never positive (an actual can't be above its forecast); in Financial Performance's chart it shows only in a bar's tooltip, not at the end of the bar, and the Clusters table shows **Forecast** (the rest of the year, Oct – Mar), **Actual** (the months that are in, Apr – Sep), **Variance** (actual − forecast over the uploaded actuals; a hyphen for someone with none), **Revenue**, **Cost** and **Target** (each the full year), **Profit** (revenue − cost) and **Margin** ((revenue − cost) ÷ revenue × 100; a hyphen with no revenue): each cluster's (its row reads **Cluster total · N people**), and each person's when a cluster is expanded: the head first, with **their own figures**, then everyone below them, so the rows add up to the cluster total. Below it, **All employees** lists everyone in the practice, one row each: the six clusters' 26 people, the six people outside every cluster (Kopal Sharma and her two reportees, Mustufa Kalabhai and the two contractors, who report to the Practice Head without a cluster of their own; their Cluster reads No cluster) and the Practice Head. It has the same figure columns (no target), plus the cluster each person is in (a link to it), billability, and the same toolbar as Resource Allocation (search, Billable | Non-billable, role, client, location, project, and how many people match). A total row adds up whoever is showing; with no filter it matches DPS Financials (revenue $1.24M, cost $953K). Every heading is one line with a note under it (the months it covers, or how it's worked out; Billable names the month its count comes from; a row of several people shows the billability bar and its count, one person's row a ✓ Yes or ✕ No, with the client it bills, or internal / bench, in the tooltip), and each row's profile button is an icon with a Show profile tooltip so the table fits the page. A cluster's people list (**Cluster breakdown**) shows the same figures as the Clusters table for each person (a cluster led below counts as one row), without the target: Forecast, Actual, Variance (a hyphen for someone with no actuals uploaded), Revenue, Cost, Profit and Margin. The trend's actual line joins a month once all its actuals are in.

The Practice Head's landing page is built around three areas, in this priority order:

## Target Gap Drill Down and Initiatives

The Gap card opens it when the forecast falls short of the target: from the Practice Overview or DPS Financials the practice, from a cluster's page that cluster (picked out on the page).

- **The Practice Head** (`#/ph/gap`): two figures, the **Target** and the **Year end current forecasted revenue** (the actuals so far plus the forecast to March), and under them how the gap adds up: **Remaining** (left to earn by March) − **Forecast** (the months to March, with what that is a month) = **Gap** (hatched red, with what each month to March needs and how many months are left; green once the forecast covers it). **Missing the mark** lists every cluster by its head's full name with its head count, the ones furthest short first: target, year-end, the gap (or how far ahead) and how many ideas its head has added. Selecting a cluster jumps to its initiatives. **Initiatives to fill the gap** groups them: practice-wide first, then each cluster under its head's full name, with how far it is short (or ahead) and an **Add for <full name>** button. The Practice Head adds an initiative for the practice or any cluster (the form lists each cluster by its head's full name and how far it's short; a name, what it's expected to add by March in US dollars, a note and a status: Planned, In progress or Done), changes or removes it, and sets the status of anything on the list. A cluster head's idea shows with a warm edge as **Idea from <full name>**; setting its status takes it up, and only then does it count toward the gap.
- **A cluster head** (a PM who leads a cluster with a target, `#/me/gap`, from Team Financials): the same figures for their cluster, **Your ideas** (add, change or remove each one until the Practice Head takes it up; the form names them and the Practice Head in full), and what the Practice Head has planned for their cluster and across the practice, read-only.
- Every addition, change, take-up and removal is in the audit trail. In the sample data the Practice Head has four initiatives (Speed up TA for open profiles and Push allocation for benched resources to billable projects across the practice, a rate revision for Kuldeep Golani's cluster and an extended engagement for Talha Siddiqui's), and Kuldeep Golani, Pooja Heda and Pratik Jaiswal each have an idea. They live in `db.gapItems`.

## Importing from CSV or Excel (Admin)

- **Import employees** sits next to Add employee on the Dashboard, People and Financial Updates. It adds new people from a sheet: Full name, Work email, Designation, Platform role, Allocation, Joining date and Reporting manager (Employee ID is optional; blank uses the next free ID). A manager can be someone else in the same sheet.
- **Financial Updates** (Admin) lists each month of the year with how many actuals are still to upload. Pick a month (any month: there are no locks) and fill its table by hand or with **Fill from CSV**: Employee ID plus any of Billability, Forecast and Actual, in ₹ lakhs. An actual above its forecast is outlined and won't save, and a CSV row that would make one is skipped. Blank cells keep the table's value, filled cells stay marked until you save, and the toast offers Undo. Nothing is saved until you choose Save changes; saved figures show in every dashboard straight away, and every change is audited. A month nobody has figures for starts from each person's latest billability. The Targets card below sets each lead's yearly target.
- Both accept a CSV file (drag it in or choose it) or cells copied straight from Excel. Every row is checked first and the preview lists what will change and why any row is skipped. `.xlsx` files aren't read directly: save as CSV or copy the cells. Downloads are blocked on the shared page, so templates are copied to the clipboard as tab-separated text that pastes into Excel as columns.
